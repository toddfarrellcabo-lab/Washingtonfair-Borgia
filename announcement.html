2026 Borgia Volunteer Fair Announcement

Washington Town & Country Fair Volunteer Information

Wednesday, August 5, 2026

The St. Francis Borgia High School Athletic Association has been the foundation for our Athletics Department for many years. With success comes hard work and sacrifice. Parents have helped the Athletic Association fund the annual athletic budget, and this year is no exception.

One of the successful volunteer-based fundraisers is the Washington Town & Country Fair soda stands and Fish & Chips stand. Volunteers are asked to help staff this event to raise funds for the 2026-27 athletic season.

Where to Report

Upon arrival at the fairgrounds, volunteers should report to the Fish & Chips stand for further instructions.

Volunteer Shirts and Parking Passes

Volunteers are asked to wear We Are Borgia t-shirts while serving.

T-shirts and volunteer parking passes will be available for pickup at the school:

  • Monday, July 27 through Thursday, July 30, from 9 a.m. to 1 p.m.
  • Monday, August 3 and Tuesday, August 4, from 9 a.m. to 1 p.m.
  • Also available at the Sports Parent Meeting on Thursday, July 30, at 6 p.m.

Schedule Conflicts

If there is a conflict with an assigned schedule, please contact another worker who may be interested in switching shifts. If help is needed finding a replacement, contact the head coach assigned to the designated schedule. Please do not contact the Main Office.

Substitute Workers

Scheduled workers are assigned from the previous year's sports rosters. Substitute Workers may be called to fill in for a scheduled worker who cannot work their assigned shift.

Fair Admission

All volunteers are required to purchase a daily ticket.

Fair admission prices are available at washmofair.com/tickets.html.
